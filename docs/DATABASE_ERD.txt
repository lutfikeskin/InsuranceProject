================================================================================
LEGEND
================================================================================

NN        = NOT NULL constraint
PK        = Primary Key
FK        = Foreign Key
(null)    = Can be NULL
1:M       = One-to-Many relationship
M:1       = Many-to-One relationship (inverse of 1:M)
──────    = Cascade delete on FK

================================================================================
KEY RELATIONSHIPS
================================================================================

1. Customer → Policies
   One customer can have many policies.
   When customer is deleted, related records handled by cascade.

2. Customer → Customer_Entities
   One customer can have many name variants (DBA, business, maiden).
   Cascade delete when customer deleted.

3. Policy → Vehicles
   One policy can have many vehicles.
   Cascade delete when policy deleted.

4. Policy → Drivers
   One policy can have many drivers.
   Cascade delete when policy deleted.

5. Policy → Coverages
   One policy can have many coverage lines.
   Cascade delete when policy deleted.

6. Vehicle → Coverages
   One vehicle can have vehicle-specific coverages.
   Cascade delete when vehicle deleted.

7. Policy → Policy_Relationships (Self-Join)
   Links policy to related policies (renewals, rewrites).
   Example: Policy #1 (2024) renewed_by Policy #2 (2025)

8. Policy → Policy_Endorsements
   One policy can have many amendments.
   Cascade delete when policy deleted.

9. Policy → Additional_Interests
   One policy can have certificate holders, loss payees, etc.
   Cascade delete when policy deleted.

================================================================================
SPECIAL NOTES
================================================================================

• Customer Resolution:
  - Anchor: customer.full_name (personal name, immutable)
  - Variants: customer_entities (business names, DBA, maiden names)
  - Matching during extraction resolves new documents to existing customers

• Policy Types:
  - personal_auto: Light schema (no compliance, focused on vehicles/drivers)
  - commercial_auto: Full schema (includes hired/non-owned, vehicle symbols)
  - general_liability: Coverage-focused (buildings, operations, aggregates)
  - other: umbrella, workers_comp, etc. (extensible)

• Coverage Ontology:
  - All coverages map to standardized codes (AUTO_LIAB_BI, UM_BI, COMP, etc.)
  - Family groups coverages (auto_liability, physical_damage, general_liability)
  - limit_* fields are legacy; new code uses per_person, per_accident, etc.

• Policy Relationships:
  - renewal: Policy #1 (old) → Policy #2 (new) [same customer, continuous coverage]
  - rewrite: Complete policy reissuance
  - mid_term_change: Coverage adjustment mid-policy
  - same_customer_new_policy: New policy, same customer (not renewal)
  - canceled_replaced: Policy canceled and replaced (often same day)
  - confidence: "confirmed" (matched by name+dates) vs "suggested" (algorithmic)

• JSON Fields:
  - field_confidences: {field_name: score} mapping
  - classification_signals: ["signal1", "signal2", ...] for audit
  - extraction_extras: Unmapped fields from extraction (extended JSON)
